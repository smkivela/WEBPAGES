Profile

Helsinki-based musician, sound designer and music producer with over 20 years of experience working with music and sound across artistic, audiovisual and performance contexts.

My work spans sound design, composition, music production, recording, mixing and live performance. I am also studying software development, with an interest in combining creative practice, web development, audio technology and software. This cv is programmed and designed by me using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
